# Advertisement Slots

The website includes ad placeholders across the homepage and dedicated pages.

Placeholder copy:
**PUT YOUR ADS HERE**
Contact Albert for more information

The slots are visual placeholders only. No ad network script or external tracking is installed by this package.

Included responsive/standard-style dimensions:
- 970x90 leaderboard
- 728x90 banner
- 320x100 mobile banner
- 336x280 large rectangle
- 300x250 medium rectangle
- 250x250 square
- 300x600 half-page
- Responsive inline slot

When connecting Google AdSense, replace the placeholder contents with the appropriate AdSense unit markup and use the size/configuration approved for the actual ad unit.
