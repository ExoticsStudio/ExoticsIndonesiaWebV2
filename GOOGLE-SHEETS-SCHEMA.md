# Google Sheets Content Schema

## NEWS

id | slug | title | image_url | published_at | category | excerpt | content | author | status

## REVIEWS

id | slug | title | score | max_score | verdict | category | image_url | excerpt | content | published_at | status

## GAME_GUIDES

id | slug | game | title | category | image_url | excerpt | content | published_at | author | status

## GAMES

id | slug | title | cover_url | genre | platform | excerpt | status

## FEATURED

id | title | youtube_id | thumbnail_url | published_at | description | status

## MERCH

id | slug | title | image_url | price | status

## STORE

id | sku | title | type | image_url | price | currency | platform | region | stock_status | product_url | status

All frontend access is GET/read-only.
